# Endcord Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as contributors, maintainers, and community members of **Endcord**, are dedicated to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, friendly, diverse, and healthy community where developers, designers, translators, and users can collaborate with enthusiasm and mutual respect.

---

## Our Standards

### Positive Behaviors We Encourage:
- **Kindness & Empathy:** Treat fellow developers and users with patience and consideration.
- **Constructive Collaboration:** Give and graciously accept actionable feedback and code reviews.
- **Respect for Differing Perspectives:** Debate ideas and technical implementations constructively without attacking individuals.
- **Focus on What Is Best for the Community:** Prioritize user privacy, performance, safety, and stability over personal convenience.
- **Transparency & Honesty:** Share context, credit contributors, and admit mistakes promptly.

### Unacceptable Behaviors:
- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information (such as physical address, private contact info, IP addresses, or Discord tokens) without their explicit consent (doxxing).
- Disruption of development channels, issues, pull requests, or Discord support channels through spam, bad-faith arguments, or brigading.
- Promoting, distributing, or requesting malicious code, exploits, raiding tools, or token grabbers.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders and project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and pull requests that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (including official GitHub repositories, issues, discussions, and pull requests), the official Endcord Discord server, and in any official public space where an individual is representing the Endcord project or its community.

---

## Reporting & Resolution

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the Endcord moderation and leadership team:
- By joining our [Official Discord Server](https://discord.gg/endcord) and opening a private Modmail ticket.
- Or by directly reaching out to the repository owners / administrators on GitHub or Discord.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. All maintainers and moderators are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

### Enforcement Guidelines

1. **Correction:** A private, written warning providing clarity on the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate.
2. **Warning:** A formal warning with consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary Suspension:** A temporary ban from interacting with the GitHub repository, issues, PRs, or Discord server for a specified period of time.
4. **Permanent Ban:** A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the project and community for severe, repetitive, or bad-faith violations.
